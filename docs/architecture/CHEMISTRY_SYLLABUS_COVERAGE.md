## Chemistry-specific gap analysis (official curriculum vs CDG)

- Buffers proper: NOT an explicit NERDC SSS 1/SSS 2 topic (checked full
  topic lists; "buffer" does not appear). pH scale exists (kc.ph_scale)
  via the Mathematics kc.logarithms cross-subject dependency
  (cl.pre56.log_ph). Buffers remain NOT_IMPLEMENTED in the CDG
  (CI-09), blocked by the Phase 15 scope constraint (new cross-subject
  dependency from Mathematics would be required). No evidence yet from
  NERDC SSS 3 (unreachable — portal 404).
- Practical work: separation techniques, laboratory safety, and titration
  are NERDC practical objectives; CDG kc.practical_chemistry covers
  titration procedure and qualitative tests only — PARTIALLY_COVERED
  pending method/item evidence extension.
- Uncovered families retained as documented deferrals: polymers,
  industrial chemistry, electrolytic-cells detail, transition metals
  (CI-09); plus newly-mapped NERDC-only topics above (Water, Air,
  Hydrogen, Oxygen, Halogens, Nitrogen, Sulphur, Chemical industry,
  Branches, Chemical hazards, Entrepreneurship).
