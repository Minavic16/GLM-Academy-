## Mathematics-specific gap analysis

Relative to the NERDC official curriculum (SSS 1–SSS 2 checked; SSS 3
unreachable), remaining NOT_COVERED topics: Bearings, Proofs of Some Basic
Theorems, Construction, Cumulative Frequency Graph (Ogive). Modular
Arithmetic, Approximation and Surds (and the two Number Base System rows)
were implemented in Phase 20 (batches 070/071/073/074) and are now COVERED.
Sets, Logic, Mensuration and Sequence & Series were implemented in Phase 17
(batches 066-069) and are COVERED at introductory level. Matrices and
determinants are PARTIALLY_COVERED via secondary JAMB/WAEC evidence
(batch 068) — not in NERDC SSS1/SSS2 topic lists; the JAMB requirement is
now officially confirmed (Phase 18, JAMB II.6, reconciled Phase 19) while
the CDG implementation evidence remains secondary (Syllabus NG, disclosed);
WAEC official remains pending and NERDC SSS 3 unretrieved. Several
NOT_COVERED rows are documented scope deferrals in the Mathematics roadmap
(ogive).

Mathematics is also the dependency root for Chemistry buffers
(kc.logarithms → kc.ph_scale, cl.pre56.log_ph) — see the cross-subject
gap register.

Official-board verification status: BOARD_UNVERIFIED — the JAMB official
UTME E-syllabus was obtained and mapped (Phase 18, reconciled Phase 19 in
JAMB_MATHEMATICS_RECONCILIATION.md), but WAEC official documents were not
